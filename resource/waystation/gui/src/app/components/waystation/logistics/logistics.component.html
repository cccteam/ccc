Logistics

Two of the quartermaster's grants live here. Receive Shipment is permitted only while a shipment has not arrived (an IS NULL condition — receiving twice is refused), and deleting an inventory lot is permitted only for lots that expired before the cutover date: fresh lots and lots with no expiry at all refuse the delete. The lots arrive sorted server-side (the reserved sort parameter, soonest expiry first).
