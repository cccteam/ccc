Requisitions

Requisitions carry the approval workflow: draft lines are editable, Submit locks them and recomputes the total on the server, and Approve is double-gated — the procurement officer's grant only covers submitted requisitions within their personal approval limit. Line unit costs are masked per cell: without the cost grant the key is simply absent and renders as an em-dash.
