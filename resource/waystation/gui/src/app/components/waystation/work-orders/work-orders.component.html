Work Orders

Work orders are stateful: the status is server-owned and moves only through the Schedule, Start, and Complete transitions below. What you see here depends on who you are — a technician sees orders assigned to their teams or authored by them, an auditor sees completed and cancelled orders only, and a station chief can delete drafts but nothing past draft. Orders sort by last activity, and Nudge flags a stalled order for attention without changing it: the touch bumps it to the top and lands in the audit trail, while every field stays exactly as it was.
