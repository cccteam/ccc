Who may enter {{ sector.value()?.name || sectorID() }}

A sector declares who may enter it. A directory is a place people sign in from, outside this application: a Google Workspace, named by its domain (example, the fleet's), or a Microsoft Entra tenant, named by its sector id. A person who signs in through a directory enters this sector only when the sector declares that directory here; one domain is one directory, and a second directory is a second declaration. Passwords are this application's own way in, and every sector admits them unless it is directory only: then its people enter through its directories alone, and a password does not reach it. A person whose every sector is directory only cannot sign in with a password at all.
