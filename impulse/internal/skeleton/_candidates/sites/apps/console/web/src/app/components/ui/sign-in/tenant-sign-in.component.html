Who may enter {{ tenant.value()?.name || tenantID() }}

A tenant declares who may enter it. A directory is a place people sign in from, outside this application: a Google Workspace, named by its domain (acme.example), or a Microsoft Entra tenant, named by its tenant id. A person who signs in through a directory enters this tenant only when the tenant declares that directory here; one domain is one directory, and a second directory is a second declaration. Passwords are this application's own way in, and every tenant admits them unless it is directory only: then its people enter through its directories alone, and a password does not reach it. A person whose every tenant is directory only cannot sign in with a password at all. Each declared directory carries a default seat: the role a stranger who signs in through it is seated with here at their first sign-in, so they enter at once. With no default seat, the directory's strangers are told this tenant admits people by invitation only, and an invited person enters with the roles the invitation names.
