Outlets Portal The portal: a second browser application on the same site

Sign in with your organization account. The directory verifies who you are; the portal decides what you may do.

Development: the server runs with the session library's skipAuth tag, which simulates the directory, so every login is APP_USERNAME from .envrc (client, a member of north). Its roles come from cmd/bootstrap/users.json. The button is the library's login component, rendered from what the server lists at user/methods; the page names no path of its own, only its own words for the directory.
