Who may enter {{ tenant.value()?.name || tenantID() }}

A tenant declares who may enter it. A directory is a place people sign in from, outside this application: a Google Workspace, named by its domain (acme.example), or a Microsoft Entra tenant, named by its tenant id. A person who signs in through a directory enters this tenant only when the tenant declares that directory here; one domain is one directory, and a second directory is a second declaration. Passwords are this application's own way in, and every tenant admits them unless it is directory only: then its people enter through its directories alone, and a password does not reach it. A person whose every tenant is directory only cannot sign in with a password at all.
